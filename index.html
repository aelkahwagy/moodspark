Talk to SparkyNew

Talk your day through.

Tell Sparky about your day out loud, no typing. He listens, answers kindly and can save it as today's note.

  • Start from your mood check-in, the Mood tab or the evening prompt.
  • Prefer to type? Switch to the keyboard anytime.
  • When you're done, save it as today's note and pick how the day felt.
  • It all happens on your iPhone, so your words stay private.

One talk a day is free. Plus makes it unlimited.

Sparky's replies are written on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence, in English and 8 more languages. In Arabic, and on iPhones without Apple Intelligence, he answers with short, warm lines of his own and keeps your words as a note. Sparky is a friendly companion, not a crisis service. If you need help, he shows you where to find it.
